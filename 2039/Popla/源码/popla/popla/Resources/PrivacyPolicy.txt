Privacy Policy for Popla

Last Updated: April 7, 2026

Popla ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection
Popla is designed to be a private experience. We do not require an account, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. 
- Local Data: Any data you create within the app (such as your urban planning checklist) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
- Media: We do not access your photo library or camera unless you explicitly grant permission for specific features.

2. Information Use
Since we do not collect personal data, we do not use it for marketing or third-party tracking. The app's primary function is to provide a curated visual and utility experience for urban lifestyle exploration.

3. Data Security
We use industry-standard measures to protect any local data stored on your device. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services
Popla may contain links to external sites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

5. Compliance with Apple Guidelines
This app complies with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines regarding user privacy and data collection.

6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app's support channels.
