Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I get and use Melon Coins? +
Every newly registered account automatically receives 20 welcome gift coins. To read exclusive elite mini-drama reviews and unlock advanced reviews, you can spend coins. If you run out, simply tap the "Get Coins" button in your Profile tab to easily top up coins via safe App Store purchases.
How do I clear local cache storage? +
If you experience slower loading or wish to release device space, go to the "Profile" tab, scroll down to "Account Configurations," and select "Clear Local Caches." This sweeps all temp imagery and log caches while keeping your tracked watchlist items securely intact.
My coin balance is not syncing correctly. What should I do? +
Please make sure you have a stable internet connection. Try terminating the app from the multitasking switcher and relaunching it. Your purchases are securely verified through official StoreKit pipelines, so balances will restore automatically once network status is active.
How can I delete my account permanently? +
We value your privacy. In the "Profile" tab, under "Account Configurations," tap "Delete Account Permanently." A confirmation alert will warn you that this action instantly wipes your profile, watch history, and coin settings permanently. Tap confirm to fully wipe your data from local systems.
