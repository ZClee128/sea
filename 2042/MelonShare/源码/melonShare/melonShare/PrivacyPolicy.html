MelonShare Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

Welcome to MelonShare ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our short drama recommendation, tracking, and sharing utility application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a premium and interactive tracking experience, we collect the following limited categories of information:

  • Account Information: When you register or log in, we collect your email address, nickname, and password to establish your user profile.
  • App Usage Data: We record your drama watchlist (dramas being tracked, completed dramas, and current episode progress) and customized recommendation cards you create.
  • Device Information: Basic device parameters (such as OS version and app version) to ensure stable operation and audio playback compatibility.

2. How Your Data is Used & Stored

Your privacy is our utmost priority. All profile details, tracking data, and customizable share cards are safely stored on your local device. We do not transmit your personal drama trackers or review drafts to external servers, ensuring you have absolute ownership over your data.

3. Age Eligibility (18+ Recommendation Compliance)

MelonShare features recommendations, reviews, and tracking mechanisms that may contain adult-themed storylines (e.g. suspense, romance, drama). By using this application, you verify that you are at least 18 years of age or have obtained appropriate parental consent in your jurisdiction.

4. Account Deletion and Data Retention

In full compliance with App Store Review Guidelines, we provide an immediate and comprehensive account deletion mechanism in the application. You can delete your account at any time by navigating to Profile > Settings > Delete Account.

Upon confirming account deletion:

  • Your email registration, profile parameters, bio, and avatar selections will be permanently erased.
  • All your local drama watchlists, episode trackers, custom-saved soundscape playlists, and sharing card designs will be permanently destroyed and cannot be recovered.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third-party advertisers or marketing entities. Your data remains fully inside MelonShare on your iOS device.

6. Audio Playback Permissions

MelonShare includes a built-in background Audio Player (Drama Soundscapes). The audio playback function runs locally using standard iOS AVFoundation frameworks. We do not access your device's microphone or record any ambient conversations.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any modifications.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to our support team at support@melonshare.com.
