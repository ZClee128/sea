Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

Welcome to Vibble. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our social networking application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address and password. You may also provide profile information such as a nickname, bio, and avatar.

User Content: We store the content you create, including posts, drama clips, and messages sent between users.

Transaction Data: For in-app purchases (Vibble Coins), transaction records are processed via Apple's App Store. We do not store your credit card information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to provide our social services, personalize your experience, facilitate messaging, and process virtual gifting transactions.

3. User-Generated Content (UGC) Policy

As a social app, Vibble allows users to share content. We implement strict moderation and reporting tools to ensure a safe environment. Users can report any content or user that violates our community standards.

4. Data Deletion

We respect your right to privacy. You can delete your account at any time within the app settings. Upon deletion, your profile, messages, and personal data will be permanently removed from our active databases.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
