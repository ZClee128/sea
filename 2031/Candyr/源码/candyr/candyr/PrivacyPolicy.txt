CANDYR PRIVACY POLICY
Last Updated: March 24, 2026

At "Candyr", we value your privacy.

1. DATA COLLECTION
Candyr does not collect, store, or share any personal information.
- No name, email, or physical address is required.
- No tracking or analytics are implemented.
- The App functions entirely without an account system.

2. COOKIES AND TRACKING
We do not use cookies or tracking technologies. All data stays local to the device where possible, though currently, the App is purely for content viewing and does not store user data.

3. THIRD PARTY SERVICES
Candyr may show content hosted on our servers, but no user-specific data is sent during these requests.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Since we do not collect any personal data, Candyr is safe for all ages (18+).
