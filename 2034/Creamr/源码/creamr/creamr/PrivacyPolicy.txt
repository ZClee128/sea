Privacy Policy for Creamr

Last Updated: March 27, 2026

1. Introduction
Welcome to Creamr ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our App.

2. Information We Collect
Creamr does NOT require a user account, registration, or login. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or device identifiers.

3. Photo Library Access
The App's Art Studio feature allows you to select photos from your device's photo library to apply artistic filters. Selected photos are processed locally on your device using Apple's CoreImage framework. We do NOT upload, transmit, or store your photos on any server. Processed images that you choose to save are written directly to your device's photo library. No image data ever leaves your device.

4. Local Data Storage
The App may save certain preferences (such as background playback settings and saved artwork collections) locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults and standard iOS storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

5. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate with any third-party social media platforms, advertisers, or analytics providers. No personal data is shared with any third parties.

6. Cookies and Tracking
We do not use cookies or any tracking technologies to monitor your activity within or outside the App.

7. Children's Privacy
Since we do not collect any personal information, our App is safe for all ages. However, the artistic content is intended for a general audience. Art content is purely creative and fictional.

8. Data Security
Although we do not collect data, we take reasonable measures to protect information stored locally on your device. We encourage you to use a device passcode to protect your personal data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective when posted in the App.

10. Contact Us
