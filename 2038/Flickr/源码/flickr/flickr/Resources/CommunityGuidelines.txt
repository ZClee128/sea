COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Fickr is a sanctuary for aesthetic expression. We believe in:

1. RESPECT FOR ARTISTRY
Respect the original vision of the "Muses" provided in our app. Our assets are curated to inspire, and we encourage creative interpretation that maintains the dignity of the visual arts.

2. HARMONIOUS EXPRESSION
When using our Studio tools, avoid creating content that is offensive, discriminatory, or promotes hatred. Fickr is dedicated to beauty and stillness.

3. INTENTIONAL USE
Our tools are designed for mindfulness and aesthetic reflection. We encourage users to use Fickr as a lifestyle tool to enhance their personal environment (via wallpapers and cards).

4. ACCOUNTABILITY
You are responsible for the presets and cards you generate. Ensure they follow your own personal and local community standards.

5. FEEDBACK & GROWTH
We value your input. If you encounter any issues or have suggestions for new "Muses", reach out to our team.

Together, we maintain the Fickr sanctuary.
