PRIVACY POLICY

Welcome to Cookr! We take your privacy seriously. This document describes how we handle information in our application.

1. DATA COLLECTION
Cookr is designed to be a local-first application. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address (unless you contact support), or phone number.

2. STORAGE
Your favorite recipes, meal plans, and application settings are stored locally on your device. We do not sync this data to our servers.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
We may use standard system frameworks (such as AVFoundation for video playback) that do not track you. We do not integrate third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

4. COOKIES
As a native mobile application, we do not use browser cookies.
