Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Welcome to Trilo ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is our top priority. This policy outlines how we handle your information and our strict adherence to user safety and age requirements.

1. Age Restriction

Trilo is strictly intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. By using this application, you represent and warrant that you meet this age requirement. We do not knowingly target or collect information from children under 18.

2. Permissions and Data Usage

To provide a premium focus experience, Trilo may request the following permissions. All data remains strictly on your device:

  • Camera & Microphone: Used solely for personalizing your workspace environment and capturing focus moments. No recordings are uploaded or shared.
  • Photo Library: Used to save aesthetic inspirations or select custom backgrounds. We do not access photos outside of your explicit selection.
  • Local Storage: Your focus history, coin balance, and settings are stored locally on your device.

3. In-App Purchases

We use Apple's secure In-App Purchase system for Trilo Coins. We do NOT collect or store your credit card information, billing address, or any financial data. All transactions are processed and secured by Apple Inc.

4. Third-Party Services

The app includes shortcuts to third-party services (e.g., WeChat, DingTalk) for your convenience. We do not share any of your focus data or personal information with these third parties.

5. No Data Collection & Tracking

Trilo does not use tracking IDs (IDFA), does not contain third-party analytics SDKs, and does not upload your data to any remote servers. Your "Focus History" is yours alone, stored only in your device's local database.

6. Security

We employ industry-standard local encryption and security measures provided by iOS to protect your local data and coin balance.
