Privacy Policy
Last Updated: June 24, 2026

Welcome to Monti. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Monti ("we", "our", or "us") manages your data.

1. Collection of Information
Monti is a local-first utility app designed for tracking cosplay stage action, stunt rehearsals, and choreography logs.
- Personal Data: We do not collect or request any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address).
- Account Creation: No account registration or profile setup is required to use Monti. All coordinates, choreography logs, and roleplay action notes are stored locally on your device.
- Local Storage: Your rehearsal records, chats, and custom configurations are saved on your own device's internal storage and are not transmitted to any external servers.

2. Media and Content
The video playing feature and community content showcase in Monti utilize cached sample data to demonstrate choreography performance. No user-recorded videos or personal media files are uploaded, shared, or processed externally by us.

3. Analytics and Cookies
Monti does not use third-party analytics tools, user trackers, or cookies to monitor your usage habits. Your actions inside the application remain entirely private.

4. Third-Party Services
This application does not contain links to external third-party services or APIs that gather user details.

5. Compliance with Apple Guidelines
Monti is fully compliant with the Apple App Store Review Guidelines. As a local utility app, we respect user sandboxing and store no telemetry data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect contact details, any changes will be posted within this document with an updated "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@montiapp.com.
