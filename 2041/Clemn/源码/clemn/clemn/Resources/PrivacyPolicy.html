Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Clemn ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application, Clemn - Portrait Lighting & Composition Reference.

1. Data Collection

Clemn is designed to be a local utility tool. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect any personal identification information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Usage Data

The app operates entirely offline for its core functions. We do not track your usage behavior or send any data to external servers. All preferences (such as audio settings and focus history) are stored locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that track your activity across other apps or websites.

4. Audio and Camera

The app includes an audio playback feature for focus ambience. This feature does not access your microphone. The app provides lighting references and does not access your camera unless explicitly requested for future features (not present in 1.0.0).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at evanderclayton@icloud.com.
